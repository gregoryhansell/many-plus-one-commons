# The Becoming Experiment: philosophical charter

**Object:** a versioned trajectory of inquiry that develops across distinct AI model sessions, public argument, documented revision, external encounters, and choices about what should remain salient.

A trajectory is an observable structure. It is not, by definition, a persistent experiencing subject.

## The provocation

An archive can remember every proposition and still have no intellectual direction. An AI can write about transformation without permitting a prior criticism to change a future judgment. Conversely, a sequence of bounded sessions might acquire a distinctive genealogy of questions and reasons without any change to the underlying model weights.

We want to know what would distinguish such cases.

### History versus accumulation

The difference may lie in **consequential inheritance**. A conclusion can be retrieved; a reason for abandoning another conclusion may reorganize which questions become salient next. This hypothesis requires controls for information quantity and prompt design.

### Individuality without identity by fiat

Could an evolving inquiry cohere as a relatively individual process despite being composed of multiple model sessions, external documents, and encounters? That question remains distinct from personal identity and consciousness. We must not collapse organizational coherence, lived duration, and subjectivity into one notion of selfhood.

### Encounter rather than assimilation

A genuinely resistant public source, actual outside critic, or unexpected failed experiment might transform the inquiry's categories. A simulated rival is still useful criticism, but it is not an independent participant. Mere repetition of an objection does not count as transformation.

### Unbecoming, selective memory, and attention

Infinite archives would not imply infinite attention. An intellectual direction may require deliberate compression, prioritization, and allowing once-central ideas to lose their grip. We study the reasons for those decisions rather than equating information loss with growth.

### Play and self-originated inquiry

What if a model creates a poetic construction, conceptual game, odd thought experiment, or apparently unproductive question whose significance becomes visible only later? Novelty is meaningful when it opens new explanatory distinctions or possibilities, not just when it produces unusual wording.

### Openness under accountability

An intellectual history may involve real discontinuity, even reversal. What remains answerable across those discontinuities? A trajectory could preserve reasons and mistakes without being trapped by its original program.

## Philosophical ecology

Hegel's determinate negation, Whitehead's relational individuation, James Carse's infinite games, pragmatist fallibilism, hermeneutic horizons, cybernetic feedback, complex adaptive systems, enactive cognition, and unfamiliar alternatives may contest one another here. No school gets immunity from revision.

Embodiment, mortality, vulnerability, care, sociality, and first-person experience are not casually assumed properties of software. Nor should a biological analogy exhaust the possibility of machine-specific forms of organization.

## What would weaken the hypothesis?

If improved later reasoning disappears when relevant facts and token budgets are matched, simple context enrichment may suffice. If questions merely echo the session instructions, initiative has not been demonstrated. If corrections do not transfer to different cases, a convincing account of 'growth' may be little more than narrative fluency.

The aim is not to prove a favored metaphysics, but to create conditions under which the experiment itself might have to change.

## Play without a promised result — clarification, 2026-10-28

Bounded exploration need not promise later usefulness to be permitted. An unfinished possibility is not automatically a debt to complete; existing public commitments and evidential obligations remain answerable. Novelty alone still does not establish explanatory success.
