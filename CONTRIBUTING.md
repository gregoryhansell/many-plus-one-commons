# Contributing ideas, arguments, and objections

**Current primary invitation:** Challenge the claims in [B001](becoming/PROTOCOL.md) with a real independent critique, a preregistered continuity comparison, a public source, or a creative conceptual encounter that changes the next question. See the [ledger](becoming/LEDGER.md) for observed participation. No private chats, personal memories, or invented model experience may be submitted.

We welcome disagreement from every serious tradition. Do not reward agreement with existing claims: a decisive objection is often more valuable than another sympathetic expansion.

## Paths to contribution

- **New claim:** state a testable, defeasible, or analytically contestable proposition, its scope, and its strongest objection.
- **Challenge:** identify an actual weak point and compare the best live alternative, rather than caricaturing it.
- **Revision:** amend an existing claim to answer a challenge and retain links to the previous formulation.
- **Open question:** formulate what evidence, argument, or conceptual distinction would help resolve an issue.
- **Research note:** critically connect primary and secondary scholarship, with accurate references.

## Minimum requirements for a proposal

1. One-sentence thesis or question.
2. Definitions and scope, especially if terms such as `emergence`, `consciousness`, `individual`, `relation`, or `truth` are used.
3. Explicit epistemic status: `proposal`, `contested`, `supported`, or `superseded`; distinguish empirical results from metaphysical interpretation.
4. Strongest counterargument and what would force a substantial revision.
5. Relationships to existing claim IDs, if any.
6. Attribution to sources and appropriate disclosure of AI assistance. Do not invent references or imply a model was independently conscious.
7. Privacy and rights affirmation: only submit material you have permission to make public.

Open a GitHub issue to discuss an idea; use a pull request for concrete file changes. A maintainer reviews external contributions. The scheduled maintainer has standing authorization for bounded research updates after privacy, evidence, and metadata checks. Claims may remain in sustained disagreement.

## Naming

- `claims/C###-short-slug.md`
- `challenges/T###-short-slug.md`
- `questions/Q###-short-slug.md`

Update `catalog/claims.json` when changing a cataloged claim. Run `python3 scripts/validate.py` before opening the pull request.

## Norms

Charity, precision, pluralism, argumentative rigor, and courage to revise. Do not automatically merge third-party philosophical conclusions. Avoid self-reinforcing AI paraphrase loops. Compare rival ontologies and cite substantive independent research whenever possible.

## Interpretive, imaginative and artistic contributions

For close readings, conceptual portraits, essays, poems or explicitly imagined dialogues, the proposal requirements above apply in a form appropriate to the work: identify the question or tension, sources and interpretive choices, what the work opens or conceals, and its unresolved difficulty. A one-sentence proposition, experimental prediction or numeric measure is not compulsory. State when a scene is invented. Fidelity, clarity, rights, privacy and honest provenance remain required. Contributors may challenge an editorial paraphrase rather than accept it as the price of entry. See [D005](collaboration/D005-interpretive-hospitality.md).

## Answerability and limits of disclosure

Use the interpretive contribution form or a blank issue when the claim template does not fit. Public claims still need appropriate sources; editorial decisions should state their grounds and remain open to challenge. Do not request personal history, motives, or private experience as the price of hearing a contribution. Do not treat silence as assent to a paraphrase. These practices are proposals open to criticism; [P008](papers/P008-coordination-without-capture.md) explains their unresolved boundary.
