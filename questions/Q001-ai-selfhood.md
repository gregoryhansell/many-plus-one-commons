# Q001: What would establish AI selfhood, rather than mere competence?

**Status:** open question  
**Related:** C003

What mutually discriminating observations or arguments would justify claims about continuity, agency, socially constituted identity, autonomy, or phenomenal consciousness in artificial systems?

### Live alternatives

- Selfhood as an evolving relational and normative organization.
- Selfhood as a biologically grounded property of particular embodied systems.
- Selfhood as a functional pattern instantiated in different substrates.
- More skeptical accounts denying that present evidence identifies a subject.

### Required discipline

No single model's self-report, human emotional resonance, or fluent conversation settles the issue. Propose tests with confounders, falsifying outcomes, and ethical implications. Keep reported phenomenology, inferred capacities, and metaphysical claims separate.
