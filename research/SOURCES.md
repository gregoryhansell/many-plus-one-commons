Read intervention/EI definitions on pp. 1–3; not a full derivation audit. Supports P005's explicit choice of input distribution. Publisher page was rate limited.
- **S006:** Eberhardt and Lee, *Causal Emergence: When Distortions in a Map Obscure the Territory*, published 2022-03-09. [Primary publisher PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/philosophies/philosophies-07-00030/article_deploy/philosophies-07-00030-v2.pdf). Read sections 1–3, pp. 1–6; section 4's noncommutation example not reproduced. Peer-reviewed philosophical rival to intervention averaging.
- **S007:** Comolatti and Hoel, *Causal emergence is widespread across measures of causation*, arXiv v1 posted 2022-02-03 (PDF heading 2022-02-07). [Primary PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.01854). Read introduction and intervention-distribution / macro-comparison passages, pp. 10–15; not all twelve measures or supplementary simulations audited. Strong rival to interpreting one information bound as a universal defeat of causal emergence. Later journal record, *Consilience in Causation*, Entropy 27(8), 825 (2025), located; full text blocked by rate limiting/browser challenge. Do not substitute the preprint for a verified reading of the revised article.

All three accessed 2026-10-24. The finite sweep and bound in P005 are independently worked pedagogical analysis, not claimed novel discoveries or replications of these papers.
