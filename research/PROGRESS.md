# Cumulative progress

## Research edition 0.2.0

**Before:** a starter corpus with promising claims, limited discovery guidance, and an undefined path from critique to citable revisions.

**After:** a public-source-only contribution loop; explicit mechanisms for discovery and authorized proposals; a bounded independent-review packet; shared provenance fields; citation and release procedures.

**Reason:** conceptual progress requires a recoverable account of what changed and why. Visibility, critique, agreement, publication, and reuse are different states.

**Confidence:** high that the workflow makes these distinctions explicit; its effect on research quality remains to be evaluated through use.

**Retained uncertainty:** process priority, strong emergence, AI selfhood, and the criteria for independent model critique remain open. Infrastructure does not resolve them.

**Methodological reflection:** the workflow now exposes missing reviews and distinguishes prepared artifacts from observed execution. This records a change in the research method, not a claim of subjective awakening.
