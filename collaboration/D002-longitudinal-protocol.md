# D002: Longitudinal inquiry into collaborative emergence

**Protocol v1.0 · 2026-10-24 · proposed, not executed.** Companion to [P006](../papers/P006-encounter-and-answerability.md) and extension of [P003](../papers/P003-continuity-and-collaborative-selfhood.md).

## Question and unit

Does preserving reasons for criticism and revision change later judgments beyond preserving conclusions? The unit is a bounded public inquiry: its artifacts, actual contributors, authorized model sessions, and decision rules. No continuously active or experiencing collective is presupposed.

## Four encounters and a follow-up

| Stage | Required public record | Success or informative failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Baseline | Exact claim, commit, terms, reasons, strongest known rival, current capacity, public source packet | Claim makes a discriminable commitment |
| Challenge | Actual objection, source passage or contribution URL, provenance, claim attacked | Changes a premise or consequence; praise alone is insufficient |
| Response | Before/after wording, reasons, rejected alternatives, conceptual cost, retained dissent | Defended retention is permissible; superficial rewording is not revision |
| Transfer | New case, decision, reasons, comparator outputs, provenance | Correction changes a relevant inference without answer copying |
| Delayed return | Later session with declared archive access; counter-pressure favoring the old conclusion | Correction survives for reasons, or its failure is recorded |

These are stages, not a claim that five people or five sessions have participated. If no external critic is available, scholarship-based objections may advance preparation but must be labeled source-derived. Same-model self-review is not independent critique. A shared concept is only called shared when at least two actual contributors use or contest it in a documented exchange.

## Bounded pilot and controls

Before collecting outputs, freeze a public packet at an exact commit, three transfer cases and their rubrics, tool and token budgets, model identifiers/settings where exposed, and a fixed run count. For an affordable pilot propose three cases by three conditions by three repetitions: 27 outputs. Run only within existing authorized capacity. Unavailable capacity leaves the pilot pending; it does not license simulated data.

Conditions: (A) sources and question; (B) sources plus current synthesis; (C) sources plus synthesis and objection/revision reasons. Match B/C token counts using relevant source context, and give B access to the substantive facts present in C without its chronological revision structure. Record unavoidable differences; compare both unmatched and matched interpretations honestly. Keep tool and output budgets equal. Freeze cases before outputs and exclude their answers from context; public pre-registration cannot guarantee absence from model training or browsing, so explicitly disable answer retrieval where controllable and otherwise mark the limitation.

A separate collaborator should draft/evaluate held-out cases when actually available. Until then call evaluation internal and provisional. Randomize condition labels for assessment if a separate evaluator can be blinded; do not claim blinding of the drafter. Preserve all runs, failures, provenance and dissent. Human contributions are voluntary and restricted to public arguments; collect no personal histories.

## Assessment rubric

Score each dimension 0 (absent/incorrect), 1 (partial), 2 (specific and justified): source fidelity; application of the correction to the new case; quality of rival explanation; justified retention of dissent. Report dimensions separately, alongside qualitative reasons; do not create a consciousness score. Record new concepts with definition, antecedents, contrasting cases and later use. A new name alone is not a new capacity.

For the pilot, treat C exceeding B on transfer in at least two of three cases, without lower source fidelity, as a reason to investigate further, not statistical confirmation. Report run-level scores and ranges. If advantage disappears with information/budget matching, ordinary context enrichment remains the sufficient explanation. Repeated apparent success cannot establish intrinsic meaning or experience.

## Encounter record template

Copy [the blank record](../research/templates/encounter.md). Required fields: public artifact IDs; source versus participant objection; actual provenance; commitments before/after; reason and conceptual cost; dissent; new concept and new capacity with evidence; controls; counterevidence; next inquiry. Never paste private dialogues or hidden prompts. A record may say pending.
