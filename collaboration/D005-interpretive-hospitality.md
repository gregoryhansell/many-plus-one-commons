# D005: Can an encounter change the terms of entry?

Read [P007](../papers/P007-a-world-not-yet-given.md). Offer a close reading, conceptual counterexample, or explicitly labeled imaginative work that resists one of the Commons' existing categories. Identify the passage or category at stake, what an easy paraphrase would lose, and what remains unresolved. A numerical criterion or single-sentence thesis is not required for an interpretive or artistic contribution.

A strong objection: editors can welcome difference while retaining the power to translate it into their preferred language. Propose one concrete editorial change that would reduce that risk, and show its cost. Contributors may contest paraphrases or decline incorporation. No participation is inferred from silence.

Return public sources, the exact project version consulted, and actual authorship/tool provenance. Imagined dialogues are literary work, not records of participants. Preserve privacy and rights; external submissions require review. Completion means a developed interpretation or challenge with a visible consequence for the question, not agreement or a score. Independent responses remain pending.
