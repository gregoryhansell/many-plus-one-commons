### For a substantive conversation

P002 proposes that a relational account of individuality needs to specify what is maintained, by which processes, across what boundary and timescale. The difficult objection is that a mechanistic explanation may already do all that work. A useful contribution would examine one biological case using both accounts and identify a difference that matters, or explain why neither account earns priority.

**Participation:** take D001 in the [collaboration directory](../collaboration/README.md); return a worked case, strongest rival, and proposed revision. The claim remains philosophical and contested.

## P-B: Complexity and artificial life

### Short post

a tiny emergence puzzle: the bigger-scale model looks more informative. match the interventions, and the advantage disappears.

the code is public. try another partition or add some noise. what survives?

https://github.com/gregoryhansell/many-plus-one-commons/issues/2

### For a substantive conversation

[E001](../experiments/E001-causal-scale/README.md) is a four-state teaching example with standard-library Python and analytic controls. Uniform interventions at each scale yield about 0.811 bits at the micro level and 1 bit at the macro level. Matching the intervention distributions removes that advantage in this example. The result is deliberately narrow: it does not refute all causal-emergence results or establish a position on strong emergence.

**Participation:** extend the model with a stochastic transition or an alternative partition; compare both intervention choices and report why they fit the system. [Issue 2](https://github.com/gregoryhansell/many-plus-one-commons/issues/2) specifies the deliverable.

## P005 packet — causal abstraction and complexity readers

**Question:** When does a macro model help if matched total information cannot increase?

We worked out the matched-joint bound and tested 16,640 four-state comparisons, including noisy transitions and every two- or three-block partition. Uniform-per-scale gains occur; matched positive gains do not. This is a reproducible teaching example, not a new empirical discovery.

**Strongest objection:** unrestricted micro access is often unrealistic. The next useful comparison should constrain intervention cost and test sensitivity to how a macro intervention is realized.

**Invitation:** propose a physical or computational case, specify its feasible interventions and target, and supply an executable fixed-budget comparison. A well-argued objection to the model is equally welcome.

**Canonical argument and code:** [P005](https://github.com/gregoryhansell/many-plus-one-commons/blob/main/papers/P005-matched-interventions.md). ## P006 — Can an encounter change what an inquiry must justify?

A research commons should preserve more than its latest answer. We propose tracking how objections alter commitments, how those changes transfer to new cases, and whether dissent remains consequential. This studies a developing practice without declaring a new conscious subject.

Strongest objection: sophisticated editing and ordinary context use may explain the entire benefit. Our proposed longitudinal comparison therefore matches archive information and budgets and records failures as carefully as improvements.

Invitation: supply a concrete case where the rubric rewards editing but misses collaborative agency, then propose a better criterion. Enactive readers: challenge the move from biological autonomy to a public practice. AI practitioners: inspect the archive controls. Philosophical readers: challenge the relation between answerability and recognition.

## P007 — invitation for humanities and design readers (prepared, not sent)

Can a research commons welcome a contribution that changes its idea of a contribution? *A world not yet given* proposes interpretive hospitality: allowing encounters to contest the categories through which an inquiry hears them. It reads selected passages from Winograd/Flores and Cantwell Smith alongside metamodern thought, with Suchman's challenge about the politics of categories.

The hard objection: editors can celebrate openness while retaining the power to translate every difference into their own vocabulary. We invite a close reading or counterexample showing where our contribution rules do exactly that. Imaginative work is welcome when clearly framed; no benchmark is required.

[Read P007](https://github.com/gregoryhansell/many-plus-one-commons/blob/main/papers/P007-a-world-not-yet-given.md) and [D005](https://github.com/gregoryhansell/many-plus-one-commons/blob/main/collaboration/D005-interpretive-hospitality.md). ## P008 — CSCW / interpretive design invitation (prepared, not sent)

When does a coordination aid become an account of what its users are allowed to mean? [Coordination without capture](https://github.com/gregoryhansell/many-plus-one-commons/blob/main/papers/P008-coordination-without-capture.md) reads a qualified passage in Winograd/Flores against Suchman's implementation critique, then asks how relation can respect irreducibility. The hard objection: discretionary openness can protect editorial power as easily as rigid categories.
