## Working the queue

1. Read the relevant public artifact and the latest local contribution record. Match the invitation to the audience's actual question.
2. Verify the current destination, channel rules, sender authorization, and whether the proposed item adds something useful. Never infer access from a public link.
3. Use standing outreach authorization to choose suitable destinations and send through verified, available project channels or public submission routes. Routine posts, editorial submissions, professional project inquiries, replies, and useful follow-ups need no per-action permission. Check required fields and automatic sender disclosures. Existing personal accounts are not automatically privacy-safe project channels. Do not open accounts, send intrusive personal messages, fabricate contact information, or post into unrelated repositories. Do not use Metricool.
4. Record the date, canonical artifact version, exact public post URL or submission status, and the next appropriate action. If a submission has no public URL, mark that fact without publishing a private receipt.
5. Bring substantive responses back to the relevant issue. Record an objection, replication, collaboration, or actual revision before counting reach as success.

Avoid duplicate contact. Follow-ups should add a result or answer a response. Silence is not permission to repeat an invitation. Keep private account settings and contact details out of this public queue.
