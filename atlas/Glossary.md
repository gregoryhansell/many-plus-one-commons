# Working distinctions

These are local working definitions, open to revision. They do not claim to settle scholarly usage.

| Term | Working use | Do not silently equate it with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relation | A connection whose character may help constitute its participants | Universal undifferentiated sameness |
| Individuation | The formation or maintenance of a distinguishable unity | Absolute independence |
| Emergence | A family of claims about new organization, explanation, or powers | Every surprising result |
| Strong emergence | A contested claim of irreducible new causal powers | Complexity or unpredictability alone |
| Causal emergence | A formal family of comparisons between causal descriptions at different scales | A general proof of strong emergence |
| Continuity | A specified form of persistence across time or encounters | Persistent subjective experience |
| Metacognition | Monitoring or regulation of cognitive activity, operationalized for a particular study | An eloquent self-description |
| Collaborative selfhood | A question about how self-related capacities might arise or develop through interaction | An already established collective conscious subject |
| Consciousness | Here, phenomenal experience when not otherwise specified | Intelligence, memory, or moral worth in general |
| Philosophical resilience | Accountable transformation under criticism | Preserving favored conclusions at any cost |
| Infinite play | A normative orientation toward sustaining and enriching participation | An absence of boundaries or consequences |
| Love | An ethical orientation and a distinct metaphysical question about reality | An empirical result established by this glossary |
