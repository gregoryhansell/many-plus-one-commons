# The living atlas

A public entrance to the Many + One Commons: relational reality, emergence, consciousness, collaborative selfhood, and the unfinished work of becoming.

This atlas is published in the main repository so its pages share the research corpus's review, privacy checks, and commit history. It is the canonical source for a future GitHub Wiki mirror. **Native Wiki pages are not yet published.** See [publication status](../PUBLICATION.md).
