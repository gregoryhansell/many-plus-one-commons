# An expanding map of questions

This is a research agenda, not a completed literature review or a closed canon. Named thinkers indicate prospective encounters; no agreement among them is assumed. A future entry must consult primary texts before attributing a specific doctrine.

| Living question | Possible encounters | Tension to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How does an individual become? | Process philosophy, Hegelian dialectic, Simondon, dynamical systems, structural realism | Constitutive relation and real distinction |
| What makes novelty consequential? | Complexity science, evolutionary theory, Bergson, Deleuze, mechanisms and causal models | Surprise, organization, and irreducibility |
| What could count as a subject? | Phenomenology, enactivism, cognitive science, panpsychism, dual-aspect theories, biological naturalism | Functional organization and phenomenal experience |
| How does meaning arise between participants? | Peirce, pragmatism, hermeneutics, Varela, De Jaegher, Di Paolo, social epistemology | Participation and disagreement |
| Can evolution bear spiritual significance? | Teilhardian thought, Whitehead, process theology, panentheism, religious naturalism | Hope, contingency, suffering, and unwarranted teleology |
| What is love's place in reality? | Theologies of love, non-coercive power, ethics of care, eros, relational metaphysics | Ethical commitment and ontological claim |
| How does inquiry stay open? | Infinite play, dialectic, fallibilism, deconstruction, Caputo, Derrida | Commitment, undecidability, and responsible action |
| What forms of collective life sustain plurality? | Commons research, Ostrom, feminist theory, Haraway, Stengers, ecological political thought | Coordination, domination, exit, and mutual dependence |
| How do networks transform knowledge and institutions? | Complexity-oriented cultural theory, Mark C. Taylor, media theory, systems research | Connectivity, fragility, concentration, and exclusion |
| What are these starting vocabularies missing? | Specific non-Western philosophies, Indigenous scholarship, disability studies, unfamiliar sciences and arts | Translation without appropriation or invented equivalence |

The last row is an obligation to do specific reading, not a claim to represent those traditions. Preserve authors' differences and permissions. Do not flatten communities into a decorative alternative to European thought.

## How the map changes

For each substantial new encounter, record the question, primary source, proposed connection, strongest incompatibility, and a concrete revision or new test. Add new rows when the question does not fit. Remove or rename a category if it conceals a meaningful distinction.

No author is required for membership. No favorite thinker is exempt from criticism. The project seeks a growing capacity to encounter, not a final inventory of correct names.
