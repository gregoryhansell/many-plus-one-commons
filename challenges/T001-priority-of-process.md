# T001: Why should processes be ontologically fundamental?

**Status:** open challenge  
**Targets:** C001

## Criticism

Dynamic relationships and persistent individuals do not, by themselves, settle the metaphysical priority of processes over substances. A sophisticated substance or structural realist can describe temporally changing entities, constitutive relations, and causal emergence.

The burden for a process-first ontology is therefore comparative: what can it explain more clearly, more economically, or with fewer unresolved puzzles than its strongest alternatives?

## Best possible process reply

Treat persistence as an accomplishment needing explanation rather than a primitive starting point. Processes and relations may offer a more parsimonious account of identity through turnover, interaction, and transformation. But an assertion of parsimony must itself be defended.

## Discriminating tests

- Compare process-first and updated substance-based explanations of biological individuality.
- Examine whether the theories differ on identity under radical component replacement.
- Test for hidden reification of relations, the "stream," or an abstract creative principle.

**Open:** This challenge is not marked resolved by popularity or by a suggestive metaphor.
