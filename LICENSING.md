# Licensing status

**No project-wide reuse license has been adopted yet.** Public publication is authorized; broad licensing is a separate decision. Do not present CC BY or MIT as already in force.

Candidates for maintainer review are CC BY 4.0 for original philosophical writing and MIT for original accompanying code. External quotations, source material, and contributions require their own rights review. Citation and public availability do not transfer ownership or guarantee permission to redistribute or adapt.

Until a license is selected, retain existing rights and seek permission for uses beyond otherwise applicable permissions. This is a project-status statement, not a claim that every form of reuse is forbidden.
